PRIVATE DESKTOP TOOL

Short-form video publishing for one account owner.

OpenShorts Local is a locally operated desktop workflow used by its owner to prepare video clips, review them, and send approved content to the owner's TikTok account.

Not a public service. Access is limited to the developer and account owner. It is not offered to third-party users.

Local operation

Video processing and application data remain on the owner's computer except when a request must be sent to an explicitly connected provider.

Owner controlled

The owner chooses which videos to upload and confirms publishing actions. The application does not operate unrelated TikTok accounts.

Limited access

TikTok authorization is requested only for the functions needed to identify the connected account and upload or publish owner-selected videos.
