Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how OpenShorts Local (the “Application”) handles information when its owner connects and uses a TikTok account.

1. Scope

The Application is a private, locally operated desktop tool. It is used only by its developer and account owner and is not available to the public or unrelated third parties.

2. Information processed

Depending on the permissions approved during TikTok authorization, the Application may process the connected account's TikTok identifier, display name, avatar, authorization tokens, posting capabilities, and status information for videos uploaded through the Application. It also processes owner-selected video files, captions, and publishing settings.

3. How information is used

Information is used only to authenticate the owner, display the connected account, upload or publish owner-selected content, report posting status, and maintain the connection requested by the owner.

4. Storage

Application settings and authorization credentials are intended to be stored locally on the owner's computer. Credentials are not placed in the public source repository. TikTok and other providers necessarily receive data sent to their services when their features are used.

5. Sharing and sale

The developer does not sell personal information. Information is not shared with advertisers or data brokers. It is disclosed only to service providers selected by the owner when required to perform requested functions, or when legally required.

6. Retention and deletion

Local application data is retained until the owner removes it. The owner may revoke TikTok access through TikTok's account settings and may delete local credentials and generated files. Data retained by TikTok is governed by TikTok's own policies and account controls.

7. Security

Reasonable measures are used to keep credentials private, including local environment files excluded from source control. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

8. Children's privacy

The Application is not directed to children and is not offered to other users. It does not knowingly collect children's personal information.

9. Changes

This policy may be updated when the Application or its integrations change. The effective date above identifies the current version.

10. Contact

Privacy questions or deletion requests may be submitted through the contact options on the developer's GitHub profile.
